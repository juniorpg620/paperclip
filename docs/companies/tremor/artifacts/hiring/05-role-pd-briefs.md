# Role PD Briefs

This document condenses the detailed position descriptions into hire-ready summaries.

## Leadership and Operations

### Founder / Engineering Lead

- Owns the sovereign network protocol, architecture, and budget discipline.
- Needs deep distributed-systems judgment, Swift concurrency fluency, and the ability to define the technical moat.
- Interview signal: can defend tradeoffs between state authority, latency, and complexity.

### Co-Engineering Lead A / Platform and Networking Lead

- Owns platform architecture, state sync, and networking discipline.
- Needs Apple systems depth, concurrency fluency, and the ability to keep the transport layer honest.
- Interview signal: can explain why the platform moat is deterministic and reviewable.

### Co-Engineering Lead B / Simulation and Sensory Lead

- Owns deterministic simulation, tactile feel, and the sensory engine.
- Needs AAA-level physics, haptics, and audio judgment.
- Interview signal: can translate feel into systems design, not just visuals.

### Technical Program Manager

- Owns RAID, Git governance, dependency tracking, and burn control.
- Needs strong technical PM instincts and comfort operating in a high-stakes R&D environment.
- Interview signal: can turn ambiguity into a concrete execution plan.

### Studio Operations and Logistics

- Owns hardware procurement, lab readiness, device inventory, and physical operations.
- Needs practical studio ops experience and high discipline around equipment and logistics.
- Interview signal: can keep a 40-device lab organized and auditable.

## Nervous System

### Systems and Network Engineer

- Owns AWDL, Multipeer, fallbacks, encryption, and low-latency transport.
- Needs Apple networking depth and a systems-programming mindset.

### Platform and DevSecOps Engineer

- Owns CI/CD, deployment hygiene, secrets, and platform hardening.
- Needs comfort with build pipelines, repo security, and release discipline.

## Sensory Engine

### DSP and Audio Engineer

- Owns ultrasonic pairing, FFT, Core Audio, and haptic synchronization.
- Needs low-level audio engineering depth and performance instincts.

### Computer Vision and AR Engineer

- Owns board ingestion, detection, tracking, and AR mapping.
- Needs applied ML, CoreML, Vision, and ARKit experience.

### GenAI Architect

- Owns search-augmented skinning, rule ingestion, and generative synthesis.
- Needs RAG, data transformation, and agentic workflow design skill.

## Fidelity and Quality

### Technical Artist

- Owns Metal shaders, PBR material fidelity, and synthetic asset generation.
- Needs real-time graphics and procedural content experience.

### Lead Product Designer

- Owns the tactile language, onboarding feel, and dual-screen interaction.
- Needs motion, haptics, and polished interaction design.

### SDET Engineer

- Owns the HIL farm, regression generation, and self-healing test coverage.
- Needs deep QA automation and platform testing experience.

### Simulation Engineer

- Owns deterministic chaos, large-scale simulation, and bug-finding loops.
- Needs large-system reasoning and high-volume test orchestration skill.

### Tools QA Engineer

- Owns latency testing, stress testing, and thermal validation.
- Needs hardware/software integration testing discipline.
