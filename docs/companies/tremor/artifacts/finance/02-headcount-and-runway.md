# Headcount and Runway

## Team Shape

The final structure is a 15-person division-based lab.

### Leadership and Operations

- Founder / Engineering Lead
- Co-Engineering Lead A / Platform and Networking Lead
- Co-Engineering Lead B / Simulation and Sensory Lead
- Technical Program Manager
- Studio Operations and Logistics

### Nervous System

- Systems and Network Engineer
- Platform and DevSecOps Engineer

### Sensory Engine

- DSP and Audio Engineer
- Computer Vision and AR Engineer
- GenAI Architect
- Technical Artist
- Simulation Engineer

### Fidelity and Quality

- Lead Product Designer
- Tools QA Engineer
- SDET Engineer

## Runway Logic

- The first $3M thesis covers the core platform.
- The expanded $4.5M plan supports the full lab and the squad-based operating model.
- If burn rises faster than planned, the TPM protects scope before it protects speed.
- If the roadmap slips, the reserve is used for targeted recovery, not broad expansion.

## Risk Controls

- Keep hiring tied to the next critical milestone.
- Do not add headcount without a named technical owner and a measurable output.
- Revisit monthly burn against the KPI dashboard and the flight plan gates.
