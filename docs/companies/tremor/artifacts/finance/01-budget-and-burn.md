# Budget and Burn

## Budget Thesis

The final Tremor plan operates as a $4.5M annual R&D lab, not a thin startup. The budget is sized to fund a 15-person team, specialized Apple hardware, a device farm, and enough runway to harden the platform before commercialization.

## Planning Assumptions

- Annual operating budget: $4.5M
- Core team size: 15
- Target burn rate: about $250k per month
- Planning horizon: 12 months
- Contingency reserve: at least $400k for schedule risk and hardware pivots

## Spend Priorities

- Senior technical headcount
- Apple hardware and test devices
- Networking, QA, and device-farm infrastructure
- GenAI and compute costs for asset generation and simulation
- Legal, studio operations, and contingency

## Budget Principles

- Spend first on the hard technical moat.
- Treat research, automation, and test infrastructure as product work.
- Keep one reserve bucket for risk recovery.
- Use the TPM to keep actual burn aligned with the flight plan.
