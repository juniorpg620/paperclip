# Investor Pitch

## One-Line Pitch

Tremor is the local-first Apple-native platform for tactile digital board games.

## How To Use

- Use this for external narrative, fundraising, and concise company explanation.
- Keep it aligned with the mission and roadmap documents.
- If the pitch drifts away from the product being built, it should be rewritten.

## Why It Exists

Digital board games usually miss one or more of these:

- low latency
- easy local joining
- tactile feel
- deterministic state
- shared living-room presence

## What Tremor Does

- Turns iPhone, iPad, Mac, and TV into one synchronized play surface
- Uses a hub for authoritative state
- Uses haptics and visual language to make play feel physical
- Uses AI to help transform content and assets faster

## Business Direction

- Premium game sales
- Expansion packs and licensed content
- Platform licensing to publishers

## Decision Owner

- Founder / Engineering Lead
