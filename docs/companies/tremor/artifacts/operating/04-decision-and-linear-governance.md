# Decision, Linear, and Approvals

## Decision Model

- Every material decision has one owner.
- Every owner writes the decision down before execution.
- Every decision records the tradeoff, the deadline, and the next check-in.
- If a decision crosses scope, budget, or architecture, it needs explicit review.

## Linear Rules

- Every meaningful task lives in Linear.
- Every blocker, dependency, and approval should be linked to the issue.
- Status updates happen in the issue, not only in the issue thread.
- Close the loop by commenting the outcome and next owner.

## Approvals

- Spend, release, hardware, and scope changes require a named approver.
- Approvals should be visible in the issue or linked doc.
- If approval is blocked, the blocker should be recorded immediately.

## Operating Default

- Prefer reversible decisions until the platform proves the core loop.
- Escalate only after the owner has written the decision and the risk.
- Keep the paper trail in Paperclip, not in private messages.
