# Investor Pitch

## One-Line Pitch

Tremor is the local-first Apple-native platform for tactile digital board games.

## Problem

Digital board games usually miss one or more of these:

- low latency
- easy local joining
- tactile feel
- deterministic state
- shared living-room presence

## Solution

- A Mac or iPad hub owns the authoritative state
- The TV is the communal board
- iPhones are tactile player clients
- Haptics and visual language make the system feel physical
- AI helps transform content and assets faster

## Business Direction

- Premium game sales
- Expansion packs and licensed content
- Platform licensing to publishers
