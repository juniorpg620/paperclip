# Mission and Thesis

Tremor is a local-first Apple ecosystem game studio focused on tactile board-game experiences.

## Executive Thesis

- Apple hardware can support a low-latency local game platform.
- The TV should be the shared board.
- iPhone and iPad should be tactile clients.
- The hub should own authoritative state.
- Haptics, audio, and motion should make digital play feel physical.

## Executive Goals

- Build a sovereign board-game platform for iPad, iPhone, Mac, and TV.
- Prove deterministic multi-device sync with physical fidelity.
- Ship a first playable slice that feels measured and dependable.
