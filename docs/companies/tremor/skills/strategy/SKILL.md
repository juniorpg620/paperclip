# Strategy

Use when Tremor needs to choose a direction, justify a spend, or make a tradeoff that affects multiple roles.

## Purpose

- Turn ambiguity into a written decision with an owner and deadline.
- Keep the $4.5M plan tied to measurable milestones.
- Make the next irreversible decision explicit before work starts.

## When To Use

- Picking the next product or platform bet.
- Reviewing a milestone, budget change, or scope reduction.
- Preparing a founder decision for the board or the studio.

## When Not To Use

- Daily issue execution.
- Lab, QA, or release checklists.
- Deep debugging or implementation work.

## Inputs

- Company goal and current roadmap.
- Budget and runway status.
- Known risks, dependencies, and blockers.

## Outputs

- A written decision.
- An owner and deadline.
- A follow-up issue or project update.

## Verification

- The decision traces back to a goal and project.
- Risk is named plainly.
- Someone is accountable for the next step.
