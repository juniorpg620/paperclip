# Company Ops

Use when Tremor needs recurring operating discipline rather than a one-off decision.

## Purpose

- Keep cadence, ownership, and follow-through visible.
- Prevent the company graph from drifting into empty process.
- Make recurring work repeatable for the whole studio.

## When To Use

- Weekly ops reviews.
- Lab hygiene, inventory, and procurement tracking.
- Writing the status artifact that keeps other roles aligned.

## When Not To Use

- Choosing the product strategy.
- Deep technical architecture decisions.
- Detailed QA or test-harness design.

## Inputs

- Current goals, projects, and open issues.
- Operating cadence, budget, and lab state.
- Escalations from the team.

## Outputs

- A clear status update.
- A follow-up issue or routine adjustment.
- An explicit owner for any unresolved work.

## Verification

- The company graph has visible next actions.
- Recurring work is actually recurring.
- Nothing critical is only living in chat.
