# Legal

Use when Tremor needs to review a term, a contract, or a risk with external obligations.

## Purpose

- Keep the studio safe on IP and obligations.
- Make vendor and contractor terms legible.
- Prevent legal surprises from living in chat.

## When To Use

- Contracts.
- IP or licensing questions.
- Vendor, contractor, or release terms.

## When Not To Use

- Internal roadmap decisions.
- Normal engineering implementation.
- Routine ops tracking.

## Inputs

- The agreement or question.
- The business context.
- Any deadlines or constraints.

## Outputs

- A legal summary or redline note.
- Open questions for counsel.
- A risk flag for the company graph.

## Verification

- The issue is named clearly.
- The recommendation is actionable.
- Follow-up owners are explicit.
