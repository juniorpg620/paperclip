# Risk Management

Use when a Tremor role needs to surface a threat, a dependency, or a decision that could slip the plan.

## Purpose

- Keep the studio honest about probability, impact, and mitigation.
- Turn vague discomfort into a named risk with an owner.
- Make escalation predictable before it becomes an incident.

## When To Use

- A dependency is slipping.
- A design choice could affect budget, schedule, or quality.
- A recurring issue needs a mitigation plan.

## When Not To Use

- For routine status updates with no actual risk.
- For implementation details that belong in the issue itself.
- For board-level strategy choices.

## Inputs

- Current milestone and owner.
- Known blocker, uncertainty, or dependency.
- Evidence that the risk is real.

## Outputs

- A named risk.
- A mitigation or decision path.
- A follow-up date for review.

## Verification

- The risk is visible in RAID.
- The owner knows the next move.
- The plan has a fallback if the risk materializes.
