# HR Operations

Use when Tremor needs to move a person through hiring or into the company graph.

## Purpose

- Keep hiring and onboarding repeatable.
- Make role expectations and process visible.
- Reduce friction for people operations.

## When To Use

- Interview coordination.
- Onboarding logistics.
- People-process updates.

## When Not To Use

- Technical design.
- Budget planning.
- Individual issue execution.

## Inputs

- Candidate or employee stage.
- Role requirements.
- Current hiring process state.

## Outputs

- A hiring or onboarding action.
- A checklist or follow-up.
- Clear ownership for next steps.

## Verification

- The process is legible to the team.
- The next action is explicit.
- Sensitive details are handled cleanly.
