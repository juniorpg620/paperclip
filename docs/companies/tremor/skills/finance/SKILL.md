# Finance

Use when Tremor needs to decide how money supports the plan.

## Purpose

- Keep spend tied to milestones and runway.
- Make tradeoffs visible before cash becomes a constraint.
- Give the founder a clear budget picture for decisions.

## When To Use

- Budget reviews.
- Headcount planning.
- Runway or burn questions.

## When Not To Use

- Product strategy.
- Individual issue execution.
- Purely technical architecture choices.

## Inputs

- Current budget and burn.
- Hiring and tooling needs.
- Milestones that require spend.

## Outputs

- A spend recommendation.
- A budget update or guardrail.
- A clear next checkpoint.

## Verification

- Spend is tied to an outcome.
- Runway impact is explicit.
- No major cost is unowned.
