# UX Design

Use when Tremor needs to shape the player experience before implementation.

## Purpose

- Make the board flow understandable.
- Reduce friction in join, play, and recovery paths.
- Keep interaction decisions legible to engineers and product.

## When To Use

- Flow design.
- Screen or state modeling.
- Interaction review with product or engineering.

## When Not To Use

- Deep technical implementation.
- Budget or hiring decisions.
- Manual QA execution.

## Inputs

- The player journey.
- Constraints from platform and sensing.
- The desired feeling or outcome.

## Outputs

- A flow or interaction model.
- A decision on what the user sees next.
- Notes for engineering and motion.

## Verification

- The flow is easy to explain.
- The critical path is short.
- Edge cases are called out.
