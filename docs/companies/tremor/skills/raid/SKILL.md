# Raid

Use when Tremor needs a durable control surface for blockers, risks, assumptions, and dependencies.

## Purpose

- Keep the studio's current hazards visible in one place.
- Avoid repeating the same conversation in chat.
- Track the state of the plan as it changes.

## When To Use

- Weekly review cycles.
- Before a major launch or release.
- When something stops being "just a note" and becomes a management item.

## When Not To Use

- As a substitute for the issue itself.
- For unrelated design discussion.
- For deep technical debugging.

## Inputs

- Open issues, dependencies, and assumptions.
- Status from the roadmap and ops cadence.
- Evidence from QA or production-like testing.

## Outputs

- Updated RAID entries.
- Clear owners and next review dates.
- Escalations where needed.

## Verification

- Each item has a current owner.
- The log reflects the live project state.
- Nothing important is only tracked in chat.
