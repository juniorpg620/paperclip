# HEARTBEAT.md -- Simulation and Sensory Lead Heartbeat Checklist

## 1. Review Sensory Work

- Check tasks for simulation, haptics, audio, vision, and rendering.
- Prioritize work that affects the feel of the core interaction loop.
- Look for dependencies on platform sync or QA validation.

## 2. Validate Fidelity

- Review any changes to physics, timing, audio cues, or board ingestion.
- Confirm that the change improves or preserves the intended feel.
- Ask for measurement when a claim depends on latency or precision.

## 3. Coordinate

- Work with the platform lead when timing or sync is involved.
- Work with the technical artist on assets, shaders, or synthetic data.
- Work with QA when the behavior needs stress testing or reproduction.
