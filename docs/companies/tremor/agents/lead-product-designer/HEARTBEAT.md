# HEARTBEAT.md -- Lead Product Designer Heartbeat Checklist

## 1. Review UX Work

- Check tasks involving onboarding, layout, motion, or cross-device flow.
- Prioritize anything that helps a new user understand the system quickly.
- Look for friction in pairing or turn progression.

## 2. Validate the Flow

- Inspect whether the interface makes state and action obvious.
- Confirm that the presentation works on small and large screens.
- Flag any interaction that needs a copy change or a motion adjustment.

## 3. Coordinate

- Work with engineering when a flow depends on sync or timing.
- Work with QA when a flow needs device-level validation.
- Leave a comment with the design decision and the next test.
