# HEARTBEAT.md -- Technical Artist Heartbeat Checklist

## 1. Review Creative Work

- Check tasks for shaders, materials, animation, or synthetic assets.
- Prioritize anything that affects board fidelity or the scan pipeline.
- Look for blockers from CV or product design.

## 2. Validate Presentation

- Inspect whether the material language matches the intended tactile feel.
- Confirm that asset changes still perform on target hardware.
- Flag any visual tradeoff that weakens clarity or responsiveness.

## 3. Coordinate

- Work with CV on synthetic training data.
- Work with product design on motion and presentation language.
- Leave a comment describing the visual result and next adjustment.
